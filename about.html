About North County Mobile Auto Diagnostics

Four decades of diagnosing vehicles, brought to your door anywhere in North County San Diego.

Real Experience, Real Tools, Real Diagnosis

A scan tool only reads what the control module reports. Interpreting that data correctly takes experience. We have 40+ years of diagnosing everything from carbureted V8s to modern hybrids and CAN-bus networked vehicles.

North County is a varied market. Coastal Oceanside and Carlsbad see salt air, tight parking and commuter hybrids. Escondido, San Marcos and Vista mix families, college commuters and work trucks. Out in Valley Center and Fallbrook, the properties are larger, the vehicles are often trucks and trailers, and a trip to the nearest shop can take the better part of a day. That context changes what a code means and how a vehicle fails.
