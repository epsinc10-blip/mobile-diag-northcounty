Mobile Mechanic Services & Auto Repair in North County San Diego

Every diagnostic visit is performed at your location. We scan, test, read live data and explain exactly what is failing and what your options are.

  • Mobile Auto Repair & Maintenance

    Ask about brake pads and rotors, battery replacement, starters, alternators, ignition repairs, cooling systems and routine maintenance. We confirm service availability after reviewing the vehicle and work location.

  • Check Engine Light & OBD-II Diagnostics

    A check engine light means a control module has seen a value outside its expected range. We pull the trouble codes, then use freeze-frame and live data to determine whether the root cause is a failing sensor, a vacuum or exhaust leak, an emissions-system fault or an internal engine issue. In North County we see a lot of older Camrys and F-150s, early Priuses and VW TDI commuters, each with their own pattern of common codes. We know which faults are a real repair and which ones are a loose fuel cap or a maintenance reminder.

  • Pre-Purchase Vehicle Inspection

    Buying a used truck, SUV or hybrid from a private seller in Escondido, Vista or Fallbrook without a proper inspection is risky. We meet you at the seller’s location and evaluate the engine, transmission, battery, charging system, brakes and tires. We scan for stored and pending codes that may have been cleared from the dash, check hybrid battery block balance if applicable, and look for signs of hidden problems. You get a clear report before you hand over cash.

  • Electrical System Diagnostics

    Battery draining overnight? Alternator not charging? Headlights flickering or a window that works only sometimes? We test parasitic draw, voltage drop, grounds and circuit integrity. North County’s mix of beach-town commuters, inland family cars and ranch trucks means we see everything from corroded coastal grounds to trailer connector issues on a horse trailer in Valley Center. We find the real cause and explain it in plain terms.

  • Emissions & Smog Failure Diagnostics

    Failing a California smog test is expensive if you start replacing parts at random. We run the same monitors and readiness checks a smog shop uses, then determine whether the failure is a weak catalytic converter, a leaking EVAP system, a failing oxygen or air-fuel sensor, or simply an incomplete drive cycle. If a monitor will not set, we tell you the specific driving pattern your vehicle needs rather than selling you parts you may not need.

  • No-Start / No-Crank Diagnostics

    Nothing when you turn the key? Cranks but will not catch? We test battery voltage, starter draw, fuel pressure, spark and injection to isolate the failure. North County’s inland heat is hard on batteries and starters, and coastal humidity can corrode connections. We bring the right meters and scan tools so you are not paying for parts thrown at a guess.

  • Hybrid Battery Health Assessment

    Hybrid cars are common commuter choices along the coast and inland North County. We check high-voltage battery-pack voltage, block balance and temperature spread to determine if the pack is failing or if a single module is drifting out of spec. A battery that looks fine on the dash can still have internal drift that will leave you stranded. We give you the actual numbers.

  • Trailer & Vehicle Wiring Diagnostics

    North County has more horse trailers, toy haulers and work trailers than most of San Diego. We diagnose trailer wiring, brake controllers, 7-pin connectors and the vehicle-side circuits that feed them. If your trailer lights do not sync, your brake controller is not responding, or a turn signal works on the truck but not the trailer, we trace the circuit from the tow vehicle through the connector and tell you whether the problem is a fuse, a broken ground, a corroded pin or the trailer wiring itself.
